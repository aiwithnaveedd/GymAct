## 4. About & Brand Story

### Origin Story
Founded in 2021 by two competitive strength athletes and biomechanics coaches, GymAct was born out of frustration with conventional commercial fitness clubs. Commercial gyms had become overcrowded warehouses of broken machines and passive resolutions. GymAct was engineered to be the antidote: a purpose-built training laboratory combining elite athletic equipment, intelligent coaching, and recovery science under one roof.

### Philosophy & Mission
> **"Action Over Excuses."**  
> We believe motivation is emotional and fleeting, but deliberate action creates momentum. We don’t sell passive memberships; we build resilient, high-performing human bodies through progressive overload, intelligent programming, and a high-accountability community.

### What Sets GymAct Apart
1. **Zero Wait Times (Capped Memberships):** Active memberships are strictly capped to ensure lifters never wait for a squat rack or lifting platform.
2. **Standardized Biometrics:** Every member receives a complimentary monthly 3D Body Scan (InBody 770) and Movement Screening.
3. **Integrated Contrast Therapy:** Direct transition from high-intensity training to Finnish saunas and ice plunges for optimized heart rate variability (HRV) recovery.

## 6. Services & Class Offerings

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PROGRAM           | FORMAT      | DURATION | FOCUS                            |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| ActHIIT           | Group Class | 45 Mins  | Anaerobic threshold & engine     |
| IronForge         | Small Group | 60 Mins  | Barbell strength & powerlifting  |
| 1-on-1 Coaching   | Private     | 60 Mins  | Custom periodized programming    |
| Hybrid Mobility   | Group Class | 45 Mins  | Joint restoration & flow         |
| Precision Macros  | Consultation| Ongoing  | Metabolic testing & nutrition    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Detailed Descriptions

#### 1. ActHIIT (Metabolic Conditioning)
A fast-paced, heart-rate-monitored circuit combining SkiErgs, sled sprints, kettlebells, and functional core work. Designed to maximize post-exercise oxygen consumption (EPOC) and build athletic endurance.

#### 2. IronForge (Barbell Strength)
Small group strength cohorts (capped at 6 lifters per coach). Focuses on progressive overload across the Big Three (Squat, Bench, Deadlift) plus Olympic lift variations with dedicated video form reviews.

#### 3. 1-on-1 Performance Coaching
Individualized coaching with comprehensive periodization plans, velocity-based training tracking, weekly lifestyle audits, and personalized nutrition alignment.

#### 4. Hybrid Mobility & Recovery
Low-impact restorative sessions focusing on spinal decompression, hip opening, active thoracic rotation, and guided breathwork protocols to accelerate nervous system recovery.

#### 5. Precision Nutrition & Metabolic Coaching
Clinical nutritional consultations including resting metabolic rate calculation, body composition benchmarking, custom macronutrient roadmaps, and ongoing accountability check-ins.
